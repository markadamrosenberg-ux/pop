Drag pieces onto the grid. Complete a row or column to pop it. Every so often, popping lines earns a surprise: a mystery piece that reveals a special shape when you pick it up, or, more rarely, a golden Frame Bubble. Drop the Frame Bubble anywhere to pop the whole board and clear the frame, for a big bonus, a new look and a bigger points multiplier. Every 10 frames you earn a bonus piece to use whenever you like. The undo arrow takes back your last move for 10% of your score. Undos are earned: one free in each of your first 3 games, one every 5th game, and one for every 10 frames you clear (you can hold up to 3).
